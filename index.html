Solving Real Traffic Challenges

Traditional systems rely on fixed timers and isolated incident response. DRISHTI introduces dynamic intelligence to anticipate, reroute, and clear roads.

1. Adaptive Signals

Problem Solved: Rigid fixed-timer traffic lights holding green signals for empty lanes while adjacent approaches experience heavy bottlenecks.
Cameras evaluate real-time traffic density on every approach, dynamically adjusting green light durations to clear congestion efficiently.

2. Situational Navigation

Problem Solved: Drivers blindly turning into unexpected waterlogging, road accidents, rallies, or severe construction blockages.
Flags localized hazards instantly and offers automated rerouting to quieter, unobstructed paths before drivers reach the blockage.

3. Traffic Ripple Prediction

Problem Solved: Delayed responses that fail to account for how a single blocked junction creates massive gridlocks miles away.
Predicts how a roadblock or accident will affect surrounding junctions in the next 15–30 minutes, allowing preemptive signal adjustments.

4. Moving Envelopes around Ambulances

Problem Solved: Ambulances trapped in standstill traffic, or emergency green corridors causing total chaos for all other commuters.
Creates a dynamic "moving bubble" around emergency vehicles, keeping traffic moving steadily ahead so emergency response is fast and orderly.

5. Centralised Monitoring System

Problem Solved: Siloed information between ground traffic officers, emergency teams, and municipal authorities.
A unified central dashboard allows authorities to track citywide health in real time, deploy personnel, and dispatch live instructions.

6. Citizen Reporting & Credits

Problem Solved: Blind spots outside camera coverage taking hours to identify and resolve.
Empowers residents to report hazards immediately. Active contributors earn credit points for verified reports, encouraging community participation.

7. Roadside Public Display Alerts

Problem Solved: Drivers without active navigation apps or internet access staying unaware of upcoming road hazards.
Physical signal board screens project immediate warnings and prompt drivers: "Open DRISHTI to reroute" before they hit traffic bottlenecks.
